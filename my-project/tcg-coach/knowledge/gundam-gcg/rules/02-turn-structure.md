# Turn structure, levels and costs
as_of: 2026-10-16
source: Comprehensive Rules Ver. 1.10.0 (2026-10-16) sections 2, 7, 9

## The five phases (7-1)
Start → Draw → Resource → Main → End. The active player drives the turn; the game does not move on while triggered effects are waiting to resolve (7-1-3).

## Start phase (7-2)
- Active step: all of your rested cards in the battle area, resource area and base section stand up (become active) at once (7-2-3).
- Start step: "at the start of the turn" effects resolve (7-2-4).

## Draw phase (7-3)
Draw 1 card. If the deck is empty after this draw, you lose immediately (7-3-1-1).

## Resource phase (7-4)
Put the top card of your resource deck into the resource area, face up and active (7-4-1). With a 10-card resource deck this growth stops after 10 turns unless effects add more.

## Main phase (7-5)
In any order and as often as limits allow (7-5-1):
- play a card from hand: deploy a Unit, deploy a Base, pair a Pilot, or use a 【Main】 Command (7-5-2);
- use an 【Activate･Main】 effect;
- attack with a Unit (see 03-battle.md).
You may end the main phase early, which moves straight to the end phase (7-5-5).

## Level vs cost (2-9, 2-10, 7-5-2-2)
- Lv is a threshold: you meet it if you have at least that many Resources in your resource area, active or rested (2-9-1). Your player level = number of Resources, including EX Resources (2-9-4).
- Cost is what you pay: rest that many active Resources (2-10-1).
- To play a card you need both: Lv met, and enough active Resources for the cost. A Lv7 / cost 6 card needs 7 Resources on the field and 6 of them untapped.
- Tokens count as Lv 0 and cost 0 (2-9-3, 2-10-3).
- Practical effect: high-Lv, low-cost cards are "late but cheap" (you can play them alongside other cards once you reach the level); low-Lv, high-cost cards are the reverse.

## End phase (7-6)
Four steps in order: action → end → hand → cleanup.
- Action step: starting with the standby (non-active) player, both players may use 【Action】 Commands and 【Activate･Action】 effects (7-6-3, section 9).
- End step: "at the end of the turn" effects resolve. <Repair> heals here (7-6-4, 13-1-1).
- Hand step: discard down to 10 (7-6-5).
- Cleanup: "during this turn" effects end (7-6-6). Then the turn passes.

## Action steps in general (section 9)
- Happen after the block step in every battle, and in the end phase (9-1).
- The standby player acts first, then players alternate (9-2, 9-4).
- Each option: play an 【Action】 Command, use an 【Activate･Action】 effect, or pass (9-3).
- The step ends when both players pass in a row (9-5).
- A Command with a 【Pilot】 section cannot be paired as a Pilot during an action step (13-2-4-2).
