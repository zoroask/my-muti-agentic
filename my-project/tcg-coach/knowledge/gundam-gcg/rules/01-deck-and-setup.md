# Deck construction and game setup
as_of: 2026-10-16
source: Comprehensive Rules Ver. 1.10.0 (2026-10-16) https://www.gundam-gcg.com/en/pdf/comprehensiverules_en.pdf

Notes written in our own words. Rule numbers point to the official document; read it for exact wording.

## Deck construction (6-1)
- Two decks: a main deck of exactly 50 cards and a resource deck of exactly 10 cards (6-1-1).
- Main deck: at most 4 copies of any one card number (6-1-1-3). Different art with the same card number counts as the same card.
- Resource deck: no copy limit (6-1-1-5).
- Colors: the deck is built from one or two colors only (6-1-1-2).
- Ban list and restricted list apply on top of these rules at official and sanctioned events. See banlist.md.
- Sideboard: the Comprehensive Rules do not define one. The ban list notice mentions "deck and sideboard", so check the BO3 Match Rules page for the event's sideboard policy before you plan side-in / side-out swaps.

## Setup (6-2)
- Each player shuffles both decks, then draws 5 cards (6-2-1-5).
- Redraw (mulligan): optional and only once. Player one decides first. The whole hand goes to the bottom of the deck, the player draws 5 new cards, then shuffles (6-2-1-6, 6-2-1-7). Because the old hand goes to the bottom, the new hand is drawn from the other 45 cards.
- Shields: the top 6 cards of the main deck become face-down shields (6-2-2).
- Each player starts with an EX Base token (0 AP, 3 HP) in the base section (6-2-3).
- Player two starts with one active EX Resource token in the resource area; player one gets none (6-2-4). An EX Resource is removed from the game when it is used to pay a cost.

## Winning and losing (1-2)
- A player who takes battle damage from a Unit while having no shields (and no Base) loses (1-2-2-1, 8-5-2-2).
- A player whose deck is empty at the draw loses immediately (1-2-2-2, 7-3-1-1). Deck-out is a real win route for decks that make the opponent use cards up.
- A player may concede at any time (1-2-4).

## Limits worth remembering
- Hand: discard down to 10 at the end of your turn (7-6-5-1).
- Battle area: up to 6 Units. A 7th forces you to trash one of yours; that is not "destroyed" (4-5-4, 11-4-2).
- Resource area: up to 15 Resources, of which at most 5 can be EX Resources (4-4-2).
- Base section: only one Base. A new Base sends the old one to the trash, which does not count as destroyed (11-5-2).
