# Attacking and battles
as_of: 2026-10-16
source: Comprehensive Rules Ver. 1.10.0 (2026-10-16) section 8, plus 3-5, 4-6, 5-5, 13-1

## Who can attack, and what (8-1, 3-2-4)
- Only Units attack. An active Unit that has not attacked yet may attack in your main phase.
- A Unit cannot attack on the turn it was deployed, unless an effect says so. Exception: a Link Unit can attack the turn it is deployed (3-2-6-3).
- Targets: the opposing player, or an enemy Unit that is rested. You cannot attack an active (standing) enemy Unit.

## Battle steps (8-2 to 8-6)
1. Attack step: rest your Unit and declare the target. 【Attack】 and "when this Unit attacks" effects trigger (8-2-2).
2. Block step: the defender may use one active <Blocker> Unit to redirect the attack to it; that Unit is rested (8-3, 13-1-4). Only one Blocker per attack, and the original target cannot block for itself. <High-Maneuver> on the attacker stops Blockers (13-1-6).
3. Action step: both players may use 【Action】 cards and 【Activate･Action】 effects, defender first (8-4, section 9).
4. Damage step (8-5).
5. Battle end step: "during this battle" effects end (8-6).
If the attacker or target leaves the battle early, the battle skips to the battle end step (8-2-4, 8-3-5, 8-4-2).

## Damage when attacking the player (8-5-2)
- If the defender has a Base, the damage goes to the Base. A Base at 0 HP is destroyed (8-5-2-4). The starting EX Base has 3 HP, so it soaks the first hits.
- With no Base but with shields, the damage goes to the top shield. Any damage destroys a shield (each has 1 HP). The shield is revealed; if it has 【Burst】, its owner may use it for free before it goes to the trash (8-5-2-3, 13-2-5).
- With no Base and no shields, the Unit's AP is dealt to the player as battle damage, and that player loses (8-5-2-2).
- Extra damage does not carry over to the next shield (5-5-6). <Suppression> and <Breach> are the ways to hit more than one card in the shield area (13-1-7, 13-1-2).

## Damage between Units (8-5-3)
- Both deal damage equal to their AP at the same time. A Unit at 0 HP is destroyed and goes to its owner's trash.
- <First Strike> on the attacker: it deals its damage first; if that destroys the target, the target deals none back (13-1-5).
- Recovering HP (<Repair>, "recover-hp" effects) only matters if damage persists on Units between battles; confirm the exact damage-persistence rule in section 5-5 / 11 of the official document before relying on it.

## Bases (3-5, 11-5)
- While a Base is in play, attacks on the player hit the Base first (3-5-3).
- Only one Base at a time. Deploying a new Base trashes the old one without it counting as destroyed (11-5-2), so 【Destroyed】 does not trigger.

## Practical notes for analysis
- Resting is a cost: a Unit that attacked or blocked stays rested until your next start phase, which makes it a legal target for enemy attacks. Effects that rest enemy Units ("rest-enemy" tag) therefore open them to attack.
- Effects that set your Units active again ("set-active" tag) let them attack twice or stay ready to block.
- Shields protect against lethal but feed the opponent free Burst effects; counting the opponent's Burst cards matters when racing.
